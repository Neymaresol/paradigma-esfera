# PARADIGMA ESFERA — rollout international (1.2.0-dev)

## Scope
Six UI languages initially: Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, German and Arabic. Course titles are currently in Portuguese and require professional translation and pedagogical review. Currency remains BRL: **do not present this as local pricing**.

## Commercial gates (fail-closed)
1. Validate course/service delivery, licensing and customer support for each market.
2. Validate company structure, local tax, consumer rights, data privacy and refunds.
3. Connect a regulated payment provider and verify webhooks, settlement and reconciled transfers.
4. Establish lawful marketing permissions, consent, platform policies and local ad disclosures.
5. Test staging orders end-to-end; do not count simulated orders as revenue.
6. Pilot one channel/market, measure verified conversion and positive contribution margin.
7. Only expand approved cells, up to 10 children per parent, with cost ceilings, health checks, human approval and rollback.

## Marketing
Do not publish until destination is functional and offerings are deliverable. Use educational organic content, clear pricing and opt-in leads. No automated spam, deceptive claims or fake clicks.

## Operational status
No production deployment, no live checkout, no paid advertising, no validated international sales. All examples are development-only.
